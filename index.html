AI tools I've built

Five agents I run for music work. Each one gathers its own numbers on a schedule and reports into a spreadsheet or a page.

  • A&R Superplaylist

    Scans 44 Spotify discovery playlists every day for songs that two or more lists picked up in the same week, keeps the small artists, logs them in a shared sheet with their streaming and follower numbers, and publishes a password-protected dashboard that tracks how each artist grows. Builds a private playlist of the week's finds three times a week.

  • Show list and announcements

    Gathers the next two weeks of concerts in New York, Chicago and Los Angeles, plus everything newly announced, from public listings, 19 venue calendars and the Ticketmaster Discovery API. Clears the duplicates, marks the team's own artists, and publishes one password-protected page.

  • Itinerary builder

    Turns five answers (artist, length, start and end room size, fixed dates) into a multi-year touring routing in the label's sheet format. A fuller version audits an itinerary that already exists: fills the gaps, fixes the room ladder, and researches comparable artists' venues city by city.

  • AXS resale scan

    Every three days it re-checks the standard, official resale and SeatGeek price for every upcoming show in the tracker sheet, and flags the ones where a fan gets in cheaper on resale than at face value. It reads prices only, never buys or holds a ticket.

  • Label map

    A single-page reference to who owns whom across the major labels, built from a company directory plus manual research. One self-contained HTML file with the logos inlined, and a matching one-page PDF poster generated from that same file, so the two can't drift apart.

As of Nov 22, 2026. The Superplaylist and show pages are password-protected; the label map is open.
