Resale tracker

Every 3 days · Writes back to the sheet

Reads the standard and official resale price on AXS, Ticketmaster, DICE and Etix, adds SeatGeek, and colors the rows where getting in costs less than face value. It reads prices only: it never signs in, holds or buys.
